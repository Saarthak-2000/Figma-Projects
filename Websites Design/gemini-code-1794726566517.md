# Digital World — Digital Agency Landing Page (UI/UX Design)

A modern, clean, and conversion-focused landing page UI designed in **Figma** for **Digital World**, a digital product and app development agency. The design emphasizes clear visual hierarchy, strong social proof, and structured Auto Layout responsiveness.

## Key Sections & Features

- **Navigation Bar:** Clean header featuring the *Digital World* brand identity, quick navigation links (*Project, Courses, About Us, Contact Us*), and a primary language/action CTA button.
- **Hero Section:** Asymmetric split-screen layout pairing bold typography (*"We create the best digital Products"*) and a primary *"Start Your Project"* CTA with a custom rounded-corner team collaboration hero image.
- **Social Proof / Client Strip:** Trust bar highlighting notable brand partnerships (*Facebook, Pinterest, YouTube, Google*).
- **Featured Projects Grid:** Two-column staggered portfolio showcase featuring mobile and web product mockups, brief project summaries, and *"View Project"* action links.
- **Client Testimonials:** Horizontal card carousel displaying client portraits, 5-star ratings (`4.9/5`), detailed reviews, and verified company badges.
- **Call-to-Action & Dark Footer:** High-contrast dark section (*"Let's Work Together"*) designed to drive conversions, followed by structured site navigation and social media links.

---

## Design System & Specifications

- **Tool Used:** Figma
- **Layout & Structure:** 12-Column Grid System & Nested Auto Layout frames
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary Accent: `#3284FF` (Vibrant Blue)
  - Background Light: `#F8FAFC` / `#FFFFFF`
  - Dark Section / Footer: `#000000`
  - Text Primary: `#111827`
